Explain this for someone who has never written code: $ARGUMENTS

If nothing was given after the command, explain the most recent thing that happened in this session: the last error, command, or change.

How to answer:

1. Say what it is in one or two plain sentences. Use an everyday comparison if it helps.
2. Say whether they need to do anything about it: "Nothing to do", or the exact next step.
3. If there's a next step in Claude Code, put the exact thing to type on its own line.

Keep it under 150 words. Don't change any files.
